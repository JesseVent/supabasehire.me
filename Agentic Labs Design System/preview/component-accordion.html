FAQ accordion · LaunchKit pattern
How do I use this template? ▾
Clone the repo, replace the placeholder text with your copy, upload your images, and wire up the buttons. Done.
How do I adjust the color theme? ▾
Update --agl-accent in colors_and_type.css — every dependent token is recalculated via OKLCH.
How do I publish? ▾
Static files. Drop it on Netlify, Vercel, Firebase, GitHub Pages, or your own server.
